Use centered chat bubbles for inbox empty states, with distinct first-use, no-results and all-read content and a clear-search action. Customize them through the new `empty` slot on `ConversationList` and `MessageView`, composed from `InboxEmpty`, `InboxEmptyIllustration`, `InboxEmptyTitle`, `InboxEmptyDescription`, `ConversationListEmpty`, `ConversationListClearSearch` and `useConversationListEmpty()`.
